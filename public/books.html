Biographies

Narendra Modi : The Man. The Times
(Read: Oct' 2016)
I recommend this book to all - especially those who blindly support Modi and those who blindly hate him. It presents a pretty good critique and an unbiased account of life story of a leader who is one of the most liked and most disliked leaders of our times. Modi, is the current (2016) prime minister of India and the book was written in and around 2012 when he was chief minister of a state in Gujarat and was rising as a possible candidate for national politics. Although I have difference of opinion politically with the ideologue he represents and kind of hate him for his alleged role in 2002 riots in Gujarat (which this book also critiqued in its own way - since he never really could explain his acquittal) - I find his life story motivating. His life is one of hardwork, determination, focus, patience and perseverance. All the adjectives I have used are absolutely in place. I recommend the book.

Technical

The Go Programming Language
(Read: Sep' 2016)
This book is the “K&R” for Go programming language. (one of the Authors is common to both C/Go and the books). It takes you to lot of “whys” in the decision making, make you learn by writing useful programs which make you feel good, covers all basics of the programming language and kind of makes you ready for learning all other details from online resources with strong base beneath it. Must read if you want to learn go (and did I tell how awesome the language itself is?)

Miscellaneous

Gujarat Files: Anatomy of a Cover Up
(Read: Dec' 2016)
This is a very controversial book by Rana Ayyub. Each chapter in this book has explosive details but I can't vouch for the truth of them. But even if 10% of the book is true, I really salute courage of the author in writing and self publishing a book which is against the people who are the current regime. I recommend the book for those interested in understanding how politics, bureaucracy and the system plays out in India!
